This package implements the cloning-censoring-weighting approach for target trial emulation introduced by Hernán et al. in 2016 [1]
Furthermore, the package also includes what we call the Webster-Clark limit (WC_limit) to specify the which patients should be weigthed in the experimental arm based on the treatment time, this is inspired by the paper by Webster-Clark et al. in 2026 [2]

References:
[1] Hernán MA, Sauer BC, Hernández-Díaz S, Platt R, Shrier I. Specifying a target trial prevents immortal time bias and other self-inflicted injuries in observational analyses. J Clin Epidemiol. 2016 Nov;79:70-75. doi: 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2016.04.014. Epub 2016 May 27. PMID: 27237061; PMCID: PMC5124536.
[2] Webster-Clark M, Li Y, Dell'Aniello S, Platt RW. Demystifying Clone-Censor-Weighting to Studying Treatment Initiation Windows: An Example Using Publicly Available Synthetic Medicare Claims Data. Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf. 2025 Oct;34(10):e70223. doi: 10.1002/pds.70223. Erratum in: Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf. 2026 Feb;35(2):e70334. doi: 10.1002/pds.70334. PMID: 40976652; PMCID: PMC12450615.
